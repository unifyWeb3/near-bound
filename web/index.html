NEAR testnet, open source, MIT, no backend

Your agent can ask for money. The chain decides.

Certalyn holds the money in a contract and enforces the limits you set. The agent cannot move a yocto on its own, cannot widen its own budget, and every attempt leaves a receipt with the rule that decided it.

Three accounts and eight commands

You need testnet NEAR, the near CLI and this repository. Put your own account names in, and every command below is the command to run. Testnet NEAR is free from the faucet.

    Evidence

    Every figure on this site comes from these calls. They need no key, no account and no explorer, so they run anywhere.

    Certalyn

    A spend limit for an agent that pays. The money lives in a NEAR contract, the limits live in the same contract, and the agent can do neither. Testnet only. Not audited. No real money has moved.

    The contract moves NEAR to a counterparty account with a plain NEAR transfer under the policy. A receipt proves the contract decided and the transfer settled. It does not prove a service was delivered.
